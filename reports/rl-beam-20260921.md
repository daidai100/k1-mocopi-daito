# Four-run RL beam — 2026-09-21

Implements R0–R3 from [the training analysis](training-analysis-20260921.md). Four treatments use one shared seed (42), rather than eight jobs covering the report's two proposed seeds. Each production job has an eight-hour learning-loop budget, finishing the current update and saving on expiry. These are simulation experiments, not accepted controllers or hardware deployments.

## Treatments and training data

| Run | Change from preceding treatment | GPU (PyTorch index) |
| --- | --- | --- |

Every run uses the identical frozen **18,054 original training clips**: 18,003 BONES-SEED and 51 KIT, 6,587,582 control frames, approximately 36.50 hours. No mirrors, held-out references, or controller-success filtering are added. These are admitted RL reference targets, not physics-qualified demonstrations.

R0/R1 retain take/family transition balancing. R2/R3 target 50% transition exposure to the frozen locomotion group and 50% to its complement, using estimated episode durations; actual exposure is logged. The group contains 8,469 originals: 1,671 walk, 807 run, 491 turn, and 5,500 transitions with explicit locomotion/walk/jog/run/sprint/turn source tags. All remaining 9,585 originals retain positive sampling probability.

R2/R3 reset mixture: 25% starts, 50% bounded pre-failure-bin sampling, 25% uniform interiors. One-second bins use **only this learner's training failures**, with weights bounded from 1 to 4 and 0.99 decay per update. Before a clip has failure observations, its biased component falls back to uniform interior sampling. No future reference or privileged simulator input was added to the actor. The manifest lists every training ID and locomotion selection reason.

## Beam and evaluation

The durable monitor maintains **four leading evaluated training checkpoints**, each run's independent best, and both unchanged-weight initializer anchors (position-only and PV). It ranks by clean trials, clean family breadth, fewer collision trials, completions, then lower exposure. Full per-family results and successful-reference IDs are retained. Training reward is not used for selection.

Evaluate every 250 updates / 16,384,000 transitions, including matched 16.384M, 32.768M, 65.536M and 98.304M exposures. Rolling recovery checkpoints are saved every 25 updates. Numbered milestones and final checkpoints are published atomically and **never deleted**. A failed learner does not stop its healthy peers; its last durable checkpoint is retained for evaluation. The terminal checkpoint is evaluated even when its iteration is not a regular milestone.

The 54-reference, no-reset CPU replay panel is the same frozen validation panel as the analysis. Repeated beam selection makes it a **selection/validation panel, not an unseen final test**. Record clean success, raw completion, collisions, falls and execution errors separately. No automatic production/hardware promotion occurs.
